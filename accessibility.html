Accessibility

Last updated: November 2026

Tarak Chaudhari Real Estate Prof. Corp. is committed to making this website accessible to as many people as possible, including those using assistive technology.

What we do

  • Use clear, readable text and strong colour contrast
  • Provide descriptive text alternatives for meaningful images
  • Support keyboard navigation and visible focus states
  • Respect reduced-motion preferences
  • Design for mobile, tablet and desktop screens

Ongoing effort

We aim to align with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 AA where practical and continue to improve over time.

Need help?

If you have trouble accessing any part of this site, or need information in another format, please call or text (306) 371-0190 and we’ll be glad to help.
