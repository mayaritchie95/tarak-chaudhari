Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Tarak Chaudhari Real Estate Prof. Corp. (“we”, “us”, “our”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you use our contact or evaluation forms, or reach out by phone, text or WhatsApp, we may collect your name, phone number, email address and any details you choose to share about your real estate needs.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiries and provide real estate services
  • To arrange appointments, showings and evaluations
  • To connect you with trusted mortgage, legal, staging and design partners when you request it

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service partners you ask us to involve, or where required by law. Our brokerage, Boyes Group Realty Inc., may process information as part of a transaction.

WhatsApp and third-party tools

Our forms can open WhatsApp to send your message. Communication through WhatsApp, phone carriers or email is governed by those providers’ own privacy policies.

Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you at any time by contacting (306) 371-0190.

Compliance

We handle personal information in accordance with Canada’s Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

Contact

Questions about this policy? Call or text (306) 371-0190 or visit us at 1171 8th Street East, Saskatoon, SK S7H 0S3.
