Meet your realtor

Tarak Chaudhari

Saskatoon REALTOR® with Boyes Group Realty Inc. — helping families buy, sell, build and invest with honesty, care and real market expertise.

Real estate, done the right way

I believe buying or selling a home should feel supported, not stressful. I treat every client like family and every home like my own — paying attention to the details that make the difference between a good deal and a great one.

My strength is a genuine understanding of the Saskatoon market and, in particular, the home-building process. From sourcing land and infill lots to helping draft a floor plan for a custom build, I guide clients through decisions most people only make a few times in their lives.

And because a move involves more than just the house, I bring a full team: trusted mortgage brokers, legal professionals, and in-house interior design and staging at affordable rates — all coordinated so you only have one point of contact.

How I can help

  • First-time buyers who want patient, clear guidance
  • Families buying or selling in Saskatoon
  • Clients building custom homes from the ground up
  • Investors seeking off-market and income properties

I’m proud to serve Saskatoon’s diverse community, and I’m glad to help clients in English, Hindi, Gujarati and Punjabi.
